# Executive Summary

This analysis uses synthetic data. It does not describe YouTube, Google, or any real creator-support program.

## Decision context

The analysis asks where creator engagement is strongest, where support demand is concentrated, and which creator segments may deserve deeper operational investigation.

## Findings

- Monthly active creators were stable, ranging from **643 to 668** across the 12-month synthetic period.
- Established creators had the highest year-end active retention at **94.3%**, compared with **89.6%** for Growth and **82.0%** for Emerging creators.
- Growth-tier creators recorded the highest median view growth between the first and second half of the year at **18.5%**. Emerging creators had median growth of **10.2%**, while Established creators had **6.6%**.
- Emerging creators generated **329.7 support cases per 100 creators**, versus **236.2** for Growth and **165.7** for Established creators.
- Monetization cases had the longest average closed-case resolution time at **15.4 hours**. Analytics questions were shortest at **9.5 hours** but had the lowest first-contact resolution rate at **62.3%**.
- Creator-level support volume and view growth had almost no linear relationship in this generated dataset (`r = 0.028`). Support demand should therefore not be presented as a demonstrated driver of growth.

## Recommended next investigations

1. Prioritize an operational review of Emerging creators because they combine the lowest year-end activity retention with the highest support demand per creator.
2. Review Monetization and Policy Question workflows for repeat contacts and handoffs because they have the longest average resolution times.
3. Investigate why Analytics Question cases have comparatively fast resolution but the lowest first-contact resolution rate.

## Limitations

The dataset is synthetic and observational. It does not contain randomized program assignment, actual cost data, authoritative lifetime-value definitions, or real creator outcomes. It cannot measure causal impact or real program ROI.
